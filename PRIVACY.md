# Privacy

Short version: **YouTube Declutter collects nothing and sends nothing.**

- It runs only on `youtube.com`, and only hides parts of the page with CSS. It doesn't read your history, account or what you watch.
- It stores your switch choices in Chrome's extension storage. Chrome may sync them between your own devices if you use Chrome sync. Remove the extension and they're deleted.
- It makes no network requests. There are no servers, analytics, ads or trackers.
- Its only permission is `storage`, to remember your switches.
